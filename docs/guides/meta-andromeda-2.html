🚀 ANDROMEDA 2.0

META ANDROMEDA 2.0

Meta has rebuilt the AI that decides which ad each person sees. Here is what changed, what it means for your Meta ads, and exactly what to test now.

What Andromeda is
  • Andromeda is Meta's ads retrieval engine: the first step that decides which ads even get a chance to be shown to a person. Out of tens of millions of eligible ads it keeps a few thousand, and Meta's ranking models then pick the winner.
  • It was built for Advantage+. More automation and more AI-made creatives mean far more ads competing for the same person, and Andromeda is how Meta sorts them.
  • The first version (Meta Engineering, 2 December 2024) reported +6% recall and +8% ads quality on selected segments.
What's new in Andromeda 2.0
  • On 28 October 2026 Meta Engineering published the next generation of Andromeda (the version creators call Andromeda 2.0).
  • It now scores every eligible ad and surfaces the top candidates at over one billion ads per second, a 100x lift over the 2024 system.
  • A new Retrieval Mixture-of-Experts model: several specialised "user towers" read each person's changing interests (ad engagement, context, LLM-based understanding of content, activity across Meta's apps), and every ad is routed to the experts that fit it best.
  • Ads are now read together with real-time context: where and when the ad would appear.
  • Meta says Andromeda has delivered a 2.4% increase in conversions on Facebook since 2025.
What it means for your ads
  • The AI decides who sees which ad by reading the ad itself and by watching how people react to it: watch time, likes, comments, shares, clicks.
  • Audience targeting matters much less. Meta still applies your targeting rules, but with a broad or Advantage+ audience the system finds the people on its own. Narrow interest stacks add very little now.
  • Your primary text is your targeting. The words in the ad tell the AI who it is for. "Still awake at 2 AM?" reaches a very different person than "New to the gym?".
  • Look-alike ads compete with each other. Ten near-copies of one ad give the AI only one idea to work with. Really different ads give it many doors to different customers.
What to test now (step by step)
  • Step 1: One campaign with a broad or Advantage+ audience. Let the AI find the people.
  • Step 2: Write 3 to 5 primary texts, each one for a different person or problem.
  • Step 3: Make really different creatives: UGC video, static product shot, carousel, customer review, before/after, text post, founder story, demo, offer.
  • Step 4: Give each creative a different hook: the first line of text and the first 3 seconds of video.
  • Step 5: Run the versions together (V1, V2, V3…) and judge them on results after enough spend, not after one day.
  • Step 6: Pause what does not bring results. Turn the winners into new variations and test again.
Primary text formula
  • Line 1: call out the person or the problem ("Still awake at 2 AM?").
  • Line 2: the result you promise, in plain words.
  • Line 3: proof or detail: reviews, how it works, what's inside.
  • Line 4: one clear call to action.
  • Write a separate version for every type of customer you want to reach. Each version opens a new door for Andromeda.
