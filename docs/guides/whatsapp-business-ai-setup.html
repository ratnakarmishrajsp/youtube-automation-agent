WHATSAPP BUSINESS AI: FREE 24/7 SETUP

Turn on Meta's AI salesman inside the WhatsApp Business app. It answers your customers day and night, in their own language.

What it does for you
  • Replies to customers 24/7: product, price, discount, delivery and payment questions, even at 2 AM.
  • Helps you sell: recommends products and collects customer details.
  • Speaks their language: Meta says it works in all native Indian languages.
  • You stay in control: take over any chat anytime, and choose which chats it hands to you.
  • Free and built in: it's inside the WhatsApp Business app. No coding, no extra tool.
Before you start
  • Use the WhatsApp Business app, not regular WhatsApp, and update it to the latest version.
  • Your business number must already be registered in the app.
  • Meta is rolling it out to eligible businesses step by step. Don't see it yet? Update the app and check again in a few days.
Step 1: Turn it on
  • Open WhatsApp Business → Tools tab → Business AI. On some phones it's called Meta Business Agent.
  • Confirm it's you (fingerprint, Face ID or PIN), link your account if it asks, and tap Confirm.
Step 2: Teach it your business
  • Let it learn from your past WhatsApp chats, so it picks up the answers you already give.
  • Add your catalog (products, photos, prices), a price list or other documents, and your website link.
  • Fill your business profile: hours, address, delivery areas, and your COD and return policy.
Step 3: Set the rules
  • Who it replies to: everyone, only new customers, or customers who come from your ads.
  • When it's active: for example, only after your shop closes.
  • Tone and instructions: tell it how to talk (copy-paste ideas below).
  • Topics to avoid: those chats come straight to you.
Step 4: Test it, then switch it on
  • Chat with it as a customer first. If an answer is wrong, add the right info and test again.
  • Then activate it. You can change its replies and training info anytime from Tools.
Copy-paste instructions (ideas)
  • "Reply short and friendly, in the customer's language."
  • "When someone asks about a product, share the price and today's offer."
  • "For COD, returns and delivery time, follow the policy in my documents."
  • "Never promise an exact delivery date. Share the usual delivery time."
  • "Send bulk or wholesale orders, complaints and refund requests to me."
  • "If you are not sure, say a team member will reply soon."
Good to know
  • UPI payment inside the chat is coming soon, Meta says. It isn't live yet.
  • To pause or turn it off, open the mobile app → Tools → Business AI.
  • Read Meta's terms on how chats are used by the AI.
  • Source: Meta (about.fb.com) and whatsappbusiness.com. Menu names can change as Meta updates the app.
