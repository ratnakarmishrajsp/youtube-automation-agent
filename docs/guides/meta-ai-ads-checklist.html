META AI ADS CHECKLIST

From 20 November 2026, Meta's updated terms make you responsible for what its AI creates or changes in your ads. Here is how to check every AI toggle in about 5 minutes.

What changed
  • Meta's Commercial Terms page says an update takes effect on 20 November 2026. It was first reported for 30 October; Meta moved it to 20 November.
  • The update adds an "AI Features" section. As quoted by Adweek (22 Oct 2026): Meta's AI features "may make mistakes and may not always produce accurate, complete, or suitable results", and the advertiser is "solely responsible" for what they create or change, including features Meta switched on itself.
  • If you keep running ads after the update, that counts as accepting it. No click or signature needed.
Why it matters
  • Real case: an Instagram ad from REI (June 2026) showed a bicycle with a second set of handlebars. REI said Meta had enrolled it in an AI tool without its knowledge.
  • AI text can add claims you never made (an offer, a discount, free delivery). AI images can change your product. Translations can change the meaning.
  • Meta says you can turn off individual Advantage+ features in Ads Manager at any time. There is no single master switch, so it is ad by ad.
Step 1: Check every live ad
  • Ads Manager → Ads tab → tick the ad → Edit.
  • Scroll to Advantage+ creative. It shows how many enhancements are on.
  • Turn off everything you did not ask for and did not review → Publish.
  • Left: enhancements on. Right: everything you didn't ask for turned off. Illustration from the reel; the labels in your account can differ.
Step 2: Open Advanced preview
  • In the ad's preview, open Advanced preview and its Advantage+ creative options.
  • Some enhancements only show up here. Turn off the ones you don't want, then publish.
  • Some enhancements only appear in Advanced preview. Illustration from the reel.
Keep vs turn off
  • Usually turn off unless you reviewed the result: translations, text improvements, AI-generated images and backgrounds, added site links, music.
  • Usually fine to keep: brightness and contrast touch-ups, cropping, format adaptation.
  • Simple rule: if you haven't seen it, don't let it run.
Step 3: Future ads and duplicates
  • Advertising settings → Creating ads → Creative features → untick Test new creative features. This only covers features Meta is still testing.
  • After you duplicate a campaign or ad, check the copy again: defaults can come back on the duplicate.
  • Before a sale (Black Friday, wedding season), preview what is actually running, not just the settings screen.
Keep a record
  • Screenshot your Advantage+ creative settings for each live ad, with the date.
  • Save a copy of the terms page as it reads on 20 November.
Sources
  • Meta Commercial Terms (update effective 20 Nov 2026)
  • PPC Land on the Adweek report (22 Oct 2026)
  • Date moved to 20 Nov (Meta spokesperson to Reuters, 23 Oct 2026)

Meta's AI can change your ad, but from 20 November the risk is yours. Turn off what you didn't ask for, check Advanced preview, and re-check duplicates.
