FREE INSTAGRAM AUTO-DM: SETUP GUIDE

Someone comments your keyword, they get your link in DM. Built with AI on Meta's official API. No ManyChat, no monthly bill.

What you'll build
  • Comment → DM: someone comments your keyword (like AUTO) on your reel and gets a private DM with your link.
  • Follow check (optional): the link goes only to people who follow you.
  • Runs 24/7 on a free Cloudflare account, even when your laptop is off.
  • Cost: Rs 0 a month. Meta's API is free and Cloudflare's free plan is enough for a creator.
Step 1: Professional account
  • Instagram → your Profile → ☰ menu → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account.
  • Pick Creator or Business. Both work with Meta's API.
Step 2: Meta API key (free)
  • Go to developers.facebook.com → My Apps → Create app and pick the Instagram use case (messaging and content).
  • Open API setup with Instagram login → Generate access token → log in with your professional account and allow the permissions.
  • Permissions the bot needs: instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_comments, instagram_business_manage_messages.
  • While the app is in Development mode it works for accounts that have a role on the app. Your own account is all you need.
  • That token is your API key. Keep it secret: never paste it in public code, comments or screenshots.
Step 3: Connect & go live
  • Make a free Cloudflare account (dash.cloudflare.com). It hosts your bot 24/7.
  • Open Claude, Cursor or Antigravity, paste the prompt below and answer its questions: your keyword, your link, your token.
  • The AI writes the code, saves your token as a secret and deploys it.
  • Test it: from a second account, comment your keyword on your reel. The DM comes within a few minutes.
Zero-cost tip: Antigravity + Jio
  • Google Antigravity is free to download and start with, so you don't need a paid AI tool for this.
  • On Jio? Eligible Jio users get Google AI Pro free for 18 months (Reliance Jio + Google offer). Claim it from the banner in the MyJio app.
  • Then sign in to Antigravity with the same Gmail: AI Pro gives you higher limits, so you can build the whole bot without paying anything.
  • Offer not showing for your plan? The free Antigravity tier is still enough for this project.
Copy-paste prompt
  • Build me a free Instagram auto-DM bot (like ManyChat) on Cloudflare Workers, using Meta's official Instagram API with Instagram Login (graph.instagram.com).

What it must do:
1. Every few minutes (Cron Trigger), read the newest comments on the reels I choose (GET /{media-id}/comments).
2. If a comment contains my keyword (any case), send ONE private reply to that comment: POST /{my-ig-user-id}/messages with recipient {"comment_id": ...}. Only for comments less than 7 days old. Never reply twice to the same comment.
3. Follow gate (on by default): the private reply asks them to follow me and tap a "Followed" quick reply. Every run, also read new DM replies (GET /me/conversations). When someone taps it, check GET /{their-igsid}?fields=is_user_follow_business. If true, send my link. If not, send a polite reminder (max 3 times).
4. Remember who already got the link (Workers KV or a Durable Object) so nobody gets it twice.
5. Send slowly: at most 8 messages per run, 4 seconds apart. If Instagram returns a rate-limit error, back off and try later.
6. Keep my access token as a secret (wrangler secret put IG_TOKEN). Never put it in the code.
7. Put the keyword, the link and the reel IDs in one config file I can edit.
8. Add a weekly job that refreshes my long-lived token.

Ask me for my keyword, my link and my token, find my Instagram user ID and reel IDs with the API, then write the code, deploy it with npx wrangler deploy, and tell me how to test it with a comment from a second account.
Instagram's rules (so you don't get blocked)
  • One private reply per comment, and only within seven days of the comment.
  • Don't blast. Send slowly: bursts of identical messages can get your messaging blocked for a while.
  • Meta sends comment webhooks only after App Review, so the bot checks for new comments every few minutes instead. That's normal.
  • Long-lived tokens last about sixty days. The weekly refresh in the prompt keeps yours alive.
