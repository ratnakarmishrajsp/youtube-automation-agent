SHOPIFY PRODUCT PAGE UPGRADE

The four things I added to my own product page (mahekh.in) to get more orders, and the exact prompts to add them to your store with Claude Code, Antigravity or Codex.

What you need
  • Your Shopify store admin (owner or a staff login with theme access).
  • An AI that can work on a folder of code: Claude Code, Antigravity or Codex.
  • Optional, for a live preview: Shopify CLI.
  • Good to know: Claude's one-click Shopify connector can read your store, but it can't edit your live theme. Product page changes happen in your theme code, which is why you open the theme folder in your AI.
Step 1: Connect your theme to your AI (safely)
  • Shopify admin → Online Store → Themes → the … menu on your live theme → Duplicate. This copy is your backup. Never edit the live theme directly.
  • Get the code of the copy: … → Download theme file (Shopify emails you a .zip, unzip it), or with Shopify CLI run shopify theme pull and pick the copy.
  • Open that folder as the project in Claude Code, Antigravity or Codex, then paste the prompts below one at a time.
  • Live preview while the AI works: run shopify theme dev inside the folder and open the preview link on your phone.
Upgrade 1: Estimated delivery date
  • Shows the buyer when it will arrive before they buy, plus a pincode check and a dispatch countdown. On my page: "Delivery available to [pincode] by Sat, 7 Nov".
  • You are editing my Shopify theme. On the product page, right under the price, add an "Estimated delivery" block:
- Show a date range: today plus three to five working days, skip Sundays, written like "Sat, Nov 7".
- Add a pincode checker: a six-digit input and a CHECK button. Check the pincode against a list of serviceable pincodes I will give you (keep the list in a theme setting or a JSON asset). Show "Delivery available to [pincode] by [date]" in green, or "Sorry, we don't deliver here yet" in red.
- Add a line under it: "Order within [hours]h [minutes]m to dispatch tomorrow", counting down to my daily dispatch cutoff time (a theme setting).
- Make it a block I can move or switch off in the theme editor. Mobile first. Don't change any other section.
  • Use your real courier times and serviceable pincodes. A wrong promise brings returns.
Upgrade 2: Short premium description (HTML + CSS)
  • Replace a plain wall of text with a short, designed story: a dark hero card, what's inside, how to use it.
  • Write a short premium product description for [product name] as HTML + CSS for my Shopify product page.
- Parts: a dark hero card with a two-line headline and one short paragraph, an "Inside the pack" panel, and a "How to use" panel.
- Scope all CSS under one class (for example .pd-premium) so it can't break my theme. No external fonts or scripts.
- Mobile first, easy to read on small screens, in my brand colours: [colours].
- Build it as a new section (sections/premium-description.liquid) whose text comes from section settings, and add it to the product template under the main product info.
  • Keep it short: the buyer should get the feel of the product in a few seconds of scrolling.
Upgrade 3: Sticky Buy Now button
  • A bar with the product, price and Buy Now that stays on screen while the buyer scrolls, so buying is always one tap away.
  • Add a sticky Buy Now bar to the product page on mobile:
- Fixed to the bottom of the screen with the product image, a short title, the current price and a Buy Now button.
- Show it only after the main Add to cart / Buy Now buttons scroll out of view, and hide it again when they are back on screen.
- Buy Now must use the variant and quantity selected in the main product form and go straight to checkout. Update the price when the variant changes.
- Respect the iPhone safe area, don't cover my WhatsApp or chat buttons, and hide it on desktop.
- Build it as a snippet included in the main product section, with an on/off theme setting.
Upgrade 4: Sales notifications (real orders only)
  • Small pop-ups like "Someone in Lucknow just bought this" show that real people are buying. That proof creates urgency in the buyer's mind.
  • Add sales notifications to my product page using only real orders:
- A small pop-up at the bottom left: product image, "Someone in [city] just bought [product]" and how long ago.
- The data must come from real recent orders (a sales-pop app or a feed I control). If there are no recent orders, show nothing. Never invent names, cities or numbers.
- One pop-up at a time, a few seconds each, with a close button, and never on top of the sticky Buy Now bar.
  • Easiest route: a sales-pop app from the Shopify App Store that reads your real orders.
  • Never fake it. Made-up "just bought" pop-ups or fake countdowns count as false urgency under India's dark-pattern guidelines (Central Consumer Protection Authority), and buyers stop trusting you.
Step 2: Preview, then publish
  • Check the page on your phone: the delivery check, the description, the sticky bar while you scroll, and the pop-ups.
  • With Shopify CLI: shopify theme push --unpublished uploads the work as a new draft theme. Or zip the folder and use Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip file.
  • Open Preview, test one real checkout, then Publish. Your old theme stays in the list as a backup.
