INDIA POST BUSINESS ACCOUNT: STEP-BY-STEP

Ship your online orders by Speed Post at the business (contract) rate, with COD money paid to your bank.

Who this is for
  • The cheap rates need a business account. India Post calls it a contractual customer: someone who gives Rs 10,000 of postage business in a calendar month (official India Post definition).
  • Shipping less than that? Ask your Head Post Office how they apply the rule. Local practice can differ.
  • Walk-in (counter) customers pay the higher retail rate and get no COD option.
Step 1: Create your India Post login online
  • Open indiapost.gov.in → Login → register as a new user with your mobile number and email.
  • Keep the login details India Post sends you. You'll use the same mobile and email for the business account.
Step 2: Apply for the business (contract) account
  • Contact the Business Development / Marketing team of your postal division (through your Head Post Office), or call the India Post helpline 1800 266 6868.
  • Tell them what you need: Speed Post Parcel + Cash on Delivery + pickup for your online orders.
  • My experience: the account was created online and my bank account was verified online. Ask them for the online application link.
Documents to keep ready
  • Aadhaar of the owner or authorised person
  • PAN (personal or business) and GST certificate, if you are GST-registered
  • Business or address proof: shop licence, Udyam certificate, rent agreement or electricity bill
  • Cancelled cheque or passbook copy: this bank account receives your COD money
  • A working mobile number and email
Choose how you pay for postage
  • Advance deposit: keep a balance, and each booking is deducted from it. It gets the higher volume discount.
  • Book Now Pay Later (BNPL): book now, pay a monthly bill. Usually needs a security deposit or bank guarantee.
Speed Post Parcel contract rates (from 1 Aug 2026, + GST)
  • Up to 500 g: Local Rs 28 · Within State Rs 36 · Metro/Zone Rs 40 · Other States Rs 47
  • 501 g to 1 kg: Local Rs 31 · Within State Rs 49 · Metro/Zone Rs 65 · Other States Rs 71
  • Counter (retail) price for the same 500 g parcel: Rs 28 / 76 / 82 / 90. That's why the contract matters.
  • Extra volume discount on monthly postage from Rs 50,000: 5% to 15% on credit (BNPL), 7% to 17% on advance deposit.
  • Source: indiapost.gov.in, Speed Post Parcel (Domestic) tariff. Rates can change, so check before you sign.
COD and payouts
  • Cash on Delivery is available for e-commerce sellers on a contract. The money collected from your customer is paid to your bank account.
  • Check the COD fee and the payout cycle in your agreement. On my contract there is no extra COD charge on the bill.
After you get your Customer ID
  • Quote your Customer ID on every booking at your booking office, or ask about free pickup from your premises (for eligible bulk customers).
  • Track every parcel on indiapost.gov.in. Delivery standard: local 1–2 days, metro to metro 1–3 days, rest of India 4–5 days.
  • Compensation: if it's late you get the Speed Post charge back; if it's lost or damaged, double the charge or Rs 1,000, whichever is less.
  • Complaints: raise them with your booking office or the helpline, and quote the article number.
