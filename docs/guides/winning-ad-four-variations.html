Step one: find your winning campaign
  • Open Ads Manager → Ads tab. Set the dates to the last week or two and sort by Results (purchases) and Purchase ROAS.
  • A winner is an ad that keeps bringing purchases above your break-even ROAS. Break-even ROAS = your selling price ÷ your profit before ad spend.
  • Download that ad's image or video (or copy its script). This is what you give the AI.

Step two: ask for four new winners
  • Open ChatGPT, Claude or Antigravity (any image-capable model; Gemini / Nano Banana works the same way). For an image ad, attach it and paste:
  • This is my winning ad creative. [ATTACH THE AD IMAGE]
It sells [PRODUCT] to [WHO BUYS IT]. The emotion that makes people buy is: [e.g. pride / relief / fear of missing out / a gift for someone they love].

Make four NEW ad creatives with the SAME emotion, each one a different variation:
- a different scene or setting
- a different headline on the image
- a different person or angle with the product
- a different format (close-up, lifestyle, before/after, or text-led)

Keep my product exactly as it is: same shape, colour, label and packaging.
Vertical (four by five) for Instagram and Facebook feeds. Make one image at a time.
  • For a video ad, paste this instead:
  • This is the script of my winning video ad: [PASTE WHAT IS SAID AND SHOWN, SCENE BY SCENE]
It sells [PRODUCT] to [WHO BUYS IT]. The emotion that works is: [EMOTION].

Write four new video ads with the SAME emotion, each a different variation:
a new hook for the first three seconds, a new scene, and a new way of showing the product.
Keep each one short, and end every script with the same offer: [YOUR OFFER].
  • If an image changes your product (wrong colour, wrong label), say: "Keep everything, but make the product look exactly like my photo."

Step three: test them in Ads Manager
  • Make a testing campaign (Sales objective, ABO: budget set at the ad set level).
  • Give each new creative its own ad set, with the same audience and the same daily budget, so the test is fair.
  • Don't touch them while they learn. Judge on cost per purchase and ROAS after a few days, not after a few hours.
  • Pause the ones that stay below break-even. Move the winners into your winners scale campaign as new ad sets, instead of pushing one ad set's budget up too fast.
  • Then repeat: every new winner becomes the next ad you copy into the AI.
