GEMINI 4 ARGON vs CLAUDE vs CHATGPT

Which AI model to use for which job, and where you can actually get Argon today.

WHAT GOOGLE CLAIMS
  • Google says Gemini 4 Argon is ahead of both Claude and ChatGPT.
  • In Google's own published benchmark table, Argon won 13 out of 19 tests.
  • Remember: these are vendor-reported numbers. Google tested its own model.
WHERE CLAUDE STILL WINS
  • Coding: on Terminal-Bench, Claude scores 66 while Argon scores only 57.
  • Independent ranking (Artificial Analysis): Claude is on top, and Argon is level with ChatGPT.
  • Bloomberg report: Google's own employees said Argon tops benchmarks but is weak at real-world coding. Google disputes this.
WHERE ARGON WINS
  • Price: running the same test costs more than 3x less with Argon than with Claude.
  • A strong pick for documents and research work.
WHERE YOU CAN USE ARGON TODAY
  • Gemini app: available on both the Pro and Ultra plans.
  • Antigravity: Ultra plan only right now. On a Pro account the model shows 'Unavailable'.
  • Google has not announced a date for Pro users in Antigravity. My guess: it should come soon.
⚡ Action Checklist
✓
Long documents, reports and research: try Gemini 4 Argon.
✓
Serious coding and terminal or agent work: stay with Claude.
✓

✓
On Pro in Antigravity: keep using your current models until Argon unlocks.

No clean winner. Argon for documents, research and price; Claude for serious coding.
